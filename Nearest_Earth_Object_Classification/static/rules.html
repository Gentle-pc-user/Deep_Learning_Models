Data Constraints & Rules

Valid ranges and requirements for celestial parameters parsed by the neural network.

  • Minimum Estimated Diameter: Must be greater than -1 and less than 38 kilometers.
  • Maximum Estimated Diameter: Must be greater than -1 and less than 85 kilometers.
  • Relative Velocity: Object velocity relative to Earth must fall between 200 and 246,990 km/h.
  • Miss Distance: The distance the object misses Earth must be greater than 1,000 km.
  • Sentry Object: Indicates whether the object is actively monitored by automated collision systems (True/False).
  • Absolute Magnitude: Intrinsic luminosity rating must be between 8.50 and 34.
